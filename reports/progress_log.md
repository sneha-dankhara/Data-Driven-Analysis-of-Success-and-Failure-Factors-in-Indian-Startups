### Dataset
The project uses a subset of a publicly available Crunchbase-derived startup dataset.

The selected dataset contains Indian startups with resolved outcomes:
- Closed startups are considered failures.
- Acquired and IPO startups are considered successful exits.
- Operating startups are excluded because their final outcome is not yet known.
