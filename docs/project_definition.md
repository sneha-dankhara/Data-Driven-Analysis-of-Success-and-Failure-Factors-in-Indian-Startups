# Project Definition

## Project Title
Data-Driven Analysis of Success and Failure Factors in Indian Startups

## Introduction

India has a large and growing startup ecosystem. Some startups grow and eventually get acquired or reach an IPO, while many others shut down.

In this project, I want to study real data of Indian startups and understand whether there are any common patterns among successful and failed startups. The main focus of the project will be to apply the concepts learned in Fundamentals of Data Science.

## Problem Statement

There are many factors that may be related to the success or failure of a startup, such as funding, industry, location and number of funding rounds.

The aim of this project is to analyze historical data of Indian startups and compare successful and failed startups to understand what patterns can be observed from the available data.

## Success and Failure

For this project:

- Closed startup = Failure
- Acquired startup = Success
- IPO startup = Success

Startups that are still operating are not included because their final outcome is not known yet.

## Objectives

- Understand the startup dataset and its attributes.
- Check the quality of the data.
- Identify and handle missing values, duplicates and outliers.
- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Compare successful and failed startups.
- Study startup funding, industries and locations.
- Apply statistical concepts learned in the course.
- Create useful data visualizations.
- Perform hypothesis testing where suitable.
- Apply feature engineering where useful.
- Draw conclusions from the analysis.

## Questions to Explore

1. How many startups in the dataset succeeded and how many failed?
2. Is there a difference in funding between successful and failed startups?
3. Are some industries associated with more successful startups than others?
4. Does startup location show any relationship with startup outcome?
5. Is the number of funding rounds related to startup outcome?
6. What are the major differences between successful and failed startups?
7. What useful patterns can be found in the startup data?

## Scope

The project will mainly focus on:

- Data understanding
- Data quality
- Data cleaning
- Descriptive statistics
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Probability and statistics
- Data visualization
- Correlation
- Hypothesis testing
- Feature engineering

Simple machine learning techniques may also be explored later if they are useful for the analysis.

## Limitations

The dataset does not contain every startup in India and some values may be missing.

Only startups with a known outcome are being used. Startups that are currently operating have been excluded.

The analysis can show relationships and patterns in the data, but it cannot prove that a particular factor directly caused a startup to succeed or fail.
