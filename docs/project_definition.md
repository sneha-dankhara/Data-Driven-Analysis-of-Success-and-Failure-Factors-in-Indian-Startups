# Project Definition

## Project Title

Data-Driven Analysis of Success and Failure Factors in Indian Startups

## Introduction

India has a large and growing startup ecosystem. Some startups grow and eventually get acquired or reach an IPO, while many others shut down.

In this project, we study data of Indian startups to understand whether there are any common patterns among successful and failed startups. The project mainly focuses on applying concepts learned in Fundamentals of Data Science to a real dataset.

## Problem Statement

There are many factors that may be related to the success or failure of a startup, such as funding, location and number of funding rounds.

The aim of this project is to analyze historical data of Indian startups and compare successful and failed startups to understand the patterns present in the available data.

## Success and Failure

For this project:

- Closed startup = Failure
- Acquired startup = Success
- IPO startup = Success

Startups that are still operating are not included because their final outcome is not known yet.

## Objectives

- Understand the startup dataset and its attributes.
- Check the quality of the data and handle missing or inconsistent values.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and descriptive statistics.
- Compare successful and failed startups.
- Study the relationship between startup funding and funding rounds.
- Use statistical tests to check whether observed differences are significant.
- Apply PCA and LDA to the selected numerical features.
- Use regression techniques to study funding patterns.
- Use logistic regression to study startup success and failure.
- Draw conclusions based on the results obtained.

## Questions to Explore

1. How many startups in the dataset succeeded and how many failed?
2. Is there a difference in funding between successful and failed startups?
3. Is this difference statistically significant?
4. Does startup location show any relationship with startup outcome?
5. Is the number of funding rounds related to total funding?
6. Can funding-related variables help distinguish successful and failed startups?
7. What useful patterns can be found in the startup data?

## Scope

The project mainly focuses on:

- Data understanding
- Data quality and cleaning
- Descriptive statistics
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data visualization
- Sampling and statistical analysis
- Correlation
- Hypothesis testing
- PCA and LDA
- Linear and multiple regression
- Logistic regression

## Limitations

The dataset does not contain every startup in India, and some values are missing.

Only startups with a known outcome are used. Startups that are currently operating have been excluded.

The dataset also does not include many other factors that may affect startup success, such as profitability, founder experience, market conditions and employee growth.

The analysis can show relationships and patterns in the available data, but it cannot prove that a particular factor directly caused a startup to succeed or fail.
